# Alignment handbook

## Preparing the bench

Begin with the platform powered down and the retaining latch open. Verify that
the reference card is seated evenly, then clear loose packing material from the
guide rails. Record the ambient reading before any adjustment so the next
operator can distinguish a setup change from ordinary drift.

When the indicator settles, rotate the quartz collar until its etched mark meets
the center line. Tighten the upper clamp first, confirm that the carriage still
moves freely, and only then secure the lower clamp. A second reading should
match the first within the tolerance printed on the reference card.
