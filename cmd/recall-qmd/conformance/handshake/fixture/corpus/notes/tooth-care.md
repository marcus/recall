# Tooth care appointment

## Question

Find a dental hygienist in the Riverside district who takes the Sample Dental
plan and is accepting new patients.

## Recommendation

Riverside Family Dental accepts the Sample Dental plan and had openings in the
first week of the month. Their intake form asks for a referral only for
orthodontic work.

## Alternatives

Two other practices in the district were checked. One was not accepting new
patients and the other does not take the plan.

## Notes

Brushing twice a day and flossing once is the baseline the hygienist asked
about. Nothing here is medical advice.
